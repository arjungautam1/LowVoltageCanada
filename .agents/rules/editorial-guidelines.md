# Low Voltage Canada Editorial & Media Guidelines

## Event Coverage
- **Security Canada / CANASA Events**: Focus exclusively on the premier national flagship event: **Security Canada Central** (Toronto, held in late October at the Toronto Congress Centre) rather than creating generic overviews of all regional stops (East, West) unless explicitly requested.
- **Title & Positioning**: Treat Security Canada Central as **"Canada's Largest Security Show"**.
- **Hero & Highlighting**: When covering major industry flagship events like CANASA Central, set `featured: true` to highlight them as the primary lead story on the home page.

## Homepage Hero & Story Placement Policy
- **Through October End (Launch Period)**: The "Welcome to Low Voltage Canada" story (`welcome-to-low-voltage-canada`) remains pinned as the lead hero ("The Big Picture") on the homepage.
- **Side Dispatches / Other News**: During October, all other news stories (including Security Canada Central in Toronto and industry insight dispatches) appear prominently in the side panel ("On the wire") and below in the news feed.
- **After October End (November 1 Onward)**: The homepage hero automatically features the most recent story published (`articles[0]`), so newly posted stories will automatically lead "The Big Picture".

## Thumbnail & Media Quality
- **Thumbnail Graphics**: Use high-impact, polished, photorealistic visuals. When creating banner thumbnails for major shows, prominently display punchy branding such as **"Canada's Largest Security Show"** and event name on the banner.
- Make visuals feel attractive, cinematic, and professional for Canada's low-voltage and physical security industry.
